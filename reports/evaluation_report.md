## Notes on interpreting these numbers

This dataset is synthetically generated from hand-written, urgency-tiered templates (see the dataset generator's module docstring for the full rationale) rather than scraped from a real 311-style system, since no such dataset was available to redistribute for this project. Accuracy this high is expected as a result: the vocabulary that signals urgency in the templates is consistent, which is exactly the kind of signal the lexicon and the classifiers are designed to pick up on. The realistic next step, called out in the README's Future Work section, is validating against real, messier citizen-submitted text.
